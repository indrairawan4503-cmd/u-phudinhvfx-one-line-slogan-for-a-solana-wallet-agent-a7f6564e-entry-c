# Variant C — Keys / Custody

## Final slogan

> Twelve words taped in a drawer — your Solana wallet agent remembers them, so you can forget them.

- Characters: 97 (gate: 1–140)
- Words: 18 (gate: ≤ 20)
- Single line, UTF-8.

## Concept

The seed phrase as twelve words taped in a drawer — a concrete, slightly
embarrassing human scene — and the wallet agent takes the burden: it
remembers them, so you can forget them. The remember/forget paradox is the
turn; it keeps the custody promise without a stock "not your keys" line
(the judge explicitly penalises stock crypto lines about keys).

## Iteration history (one concept, refined)

- v1: "Your Solana wallet agent keeps your keys safe, so you never have to think about them again." — plain custody promise, too stock.
- v2–v4: moved to the concrete scene — twelve words taped in a drawer — with the turn "guards the keys, so you just live your life."
- v5: "Twelve words taped in a drawer — your Solana wallet agent guards the keys, so you just live your life." (20 words)
- v6 (final): swapped "guards the keys" for the remember/forget paradox and dropped the word "keys" itself; tighter (18 words) and stickier.
